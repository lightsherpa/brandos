# BrandOS role names

Status: proposed for Oscar's approval. Prepared 15 October 2026.

These are display-name proposals for the Brand Core workflow. Keep current UI role labels until Oscar approves them. Do not change stored role identifiers, permissions, job ownership or certification authority as part of this proposal.

Stewart is the approved personal name of the certifier's support agent. It appears in the new Certify experience behind `VITE_CERTIFY_UX=1` or `true`; the role descriptions below still need approval. Keep **Brand Core** in English in every language, and spell **Stewart** identically in en/es/ar.

## English

| Internal role | Proposed name | One-line description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| owner | Brand owner | Owns the brand's decisions and can comment on or request changes to a certified Brand Core. |
| client | Brand contact | Provides brand assets, source evidence and answers to the team's questions. |
| creative director | Creative director | Guides creative decisions and reviews work against Brand Core. |
| certifier | Brand reviewer | Checks Brand Core against its evidence and makes the final certification decision in Certify. |
| support agent | Stewart · Review assistant | Helps the reviewer find evidence, spot gaps and prepare questions for the brand contact. |

## Role boundaries

- A person may act as both creative director and brand reviewer; those labels describe responsibilities and do not introduce extra accounts or new permissions.
- The brand contact supplies evidence. The Brand Core certification decision belongs to the authorized human reviewer.
- Stewart assists the human reviewer. Stewart cannot certify Brand Core or grant permissions.
- Owner feedback on a certified strategy, visual identity or voice starts a new version for review; it does not silently change the certified version.
- The existing Owner/Editor/Contributor/Viewer workspace access model remains separate from these workflow labels.
