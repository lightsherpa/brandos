Repeated reviewer findings belong in schema/rules/tests, not permissive prompts. No model or automation becomes the certifier. Tests do not establish human rights or v3 approval; the user must supply actual new references and the CD must review them. Active unfinished workers remain transient; durable completed-demo replay is the scoped fix. Signed/local email transport assets need stable hosting before sending; standalone export is the verified artifact.
