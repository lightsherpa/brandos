# Brand OS character direction v2

This revision supersedes the tall adult proportions and Brandolph identity in P05 and P25–P35 of the original generation guide. Keep the supplied office and furniture assets. Character identity concepts are separate from production animation sprites.

## References

Use the newly supplied game-character lineup for proportions and pixel treatment only. Create original agency specialists; do not reproduce the lineup's characters or costumes. Use the photographed man for Brandolph's identity: bald head, amber glasses, warm skin, strong brows and salt-and-pepper beard. His light grey sweatshirt is the initial outfit.

## Shared character prompt

```text
Create one original boutique-agency specialist in the attached short, playful pixel-game character direction. Approximately 2.5 heads tall: large expressive head, compact torso, short arms and short legs, sturdy feet, friendly adult personality. Bold dark pixel outlines, intentional square pixel clusters, limited warm color ramps, readable face and silhouette at small screen size. Preserve the specialist's approved identity, skin, hair, glasses and clothing colors; change the body proportions to this new direction. One isolated full-body neutral standing pose facing three-quarter front, empty hands, genuinely transparent PNG. No office, furniture, floor, drop shadow, labels, logos, border, smooth shading, realistic long anatomy or multiple poses. Reference lineup governs proportions and pixel treatment only; do not copy its franchise characters or costumes. Return the original full-resolution output. Native sprite dimensions and anchors will be established after a workstation fit test rather than forced by the concept render.
```

For each existing specialist, attach its supplied standing image as the identity reference alongside the new game-character lineup. Replace only the proportions and pixel treatment. For Brandolph, attach the photographed man instead of the previous dark-haired character.

## Production fit before animation

Start with the new Brandolph standing concept. Test him beside the supplied oak desk, orange chair and laptop. A shorter character needs a revised seated pose and chair fit, not a stretched image. Confirm pelvis on the seat, feet supported, hands at keyboard height and gaze directed toward the display. Generate UP/back and RIGHT working poses from that accepted fit, with character and furniture separate. Keep consistent cell anchors and pixel density throughout walking, sitting and typing frames. The old 32×64 target is provisional and must not force a tall silhouette.
